Protein After 50: A Practical Guide to Staying Strong

Protein is a building block the body uses every day to maintain muscle and support an active lifestyle. After 50, spreading protein across meals — for example with eggs at breakfast, beans or fish at lunch, and lean meat or dairy at dinner — can help you stay strong and steady on your feet. Pairing protein-rich foods with regular movement is a simple, everyday habit.
